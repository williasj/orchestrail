"Implement an LRU (Least Recently Used) cache with a fixed capacity. The cache must support get(key) and put(key, value) operations. When the cache reaches capacity, the least recently used entry must be evicted before inserting a new one. The implementation must include a method or helper that accepts a list of operations and returns the cache state after each step, then demonstrate it with a sequence of at least 12 get/put operations that exercises eviction, key updates, and cache hits."
