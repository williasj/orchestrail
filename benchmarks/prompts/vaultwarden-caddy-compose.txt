Write a docker-compose.yml for a self-hosted Vaultwarden behind Caddy as a reverse proxy with automatic HTTPS. Requirements: Vaultwarden data persisted in a named volume; Caddy config and certificates persisted in named volumes; only Caddy publishes ports (80 and 443, plus 443/udp for HTTP/3); Vaultwarden is reachable only on an internal network; the domain and admin token come from environment variables (DOMAIN, ADMIN_TOKEN) with no secrets hard-coded; both services restart unless stopped; include a healthcheck for Vaultwarden. Also provide the Caddyfile that proxies the domain to Vaultwarden, including the websocket notifications path.
